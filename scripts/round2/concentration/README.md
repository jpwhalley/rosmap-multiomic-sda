# Donor concentration and prioritisation

`structure/` builds the per-component table (top-donor share, participation ratio, run recurrence, modalities, adjusted phenotype calls, GWAS, protein measures), the SEA-AD statistics, the characterisation of the catalogue, the prioritisation and the new Data S1 columns. `check/` re-derives these with its own code. `reprioritise/` gives the prioritisation without the cross-layer criterion (the rule in the manuscript), and `ranking/` the selection of the five described components.

**Inputs**: `G_ROS_ROSMAP_DIR`, `G_ROS_SEAAD_DIR`, `G_ROS_DEPOSIT_DIR/cell_type.csv`, `G_ROS_TABLES_DIR`, `G_ROS_DATA_S1`, `G_ROS_AUDIT_DIR` (`c547/`, `gwas547/`, `gwas547_verify/`, `consensus_rna.csv`), and the outputs of `catalogue/counts/`, `covariates/`, `fixed_loading/`, `identity/`, `source_data/verify_source_panels/`.
**Outputs** (under `G_ROS_RESULTS_DIR`): `revision_analyses/{structure,verify_structure}/{out,private,cache}` (`private/` holds the top-donor positions) and `selection_review/ranking_of_the_five`.

**Order**: `structure/01_master.py`, `01b_gwas_autosomal.py`, `02_seaad.py`, `03_crosslayer.py`, `04_characterise.py`, `05_prioritise.py`, `07_dataS1.py`; `check/v01_core.py` to `v05_pheno.py`, then `v07_prioritise_verify.py` and `v11_tierA_fragility.py`; `reprioritise/p4_reprioritise.py`; `ranking/01_rank.py`, `02_ties.py`.

**Manuscript** (Results: Decomposition structure; Methods: Validation, Donor concentration and component prioritization; Figure S1): 193 distributed, 76 few-donor and 278 donor-concentrated components; a Gaussian score reaches a top-donor share of 0.10 in 0.005% of 20,000 simulations; 9 components between 0.50 and 0.80 and 269 above 0.80; 60 of the 61 components with three or more modalities donor-concentrated; 539 of 540 layers assayed in the dominant donor; 246 of 278 against 31 of 193 in all ten runs; 52 of 73 associations on distributed components (`04_characterise.py`). SEA-AD Spearman, 2,000-permutation null and the replication criterion (`02_seaad.py`). 113 prioritised components: 34 with a phenotype association, 1 with a GWAS hit, 96 replicated in SEA-AD; 35 with phenotype or genetic support and 78 with SEA-AD support alone (`reprioritise/p4_reprioritise.py`); all five prioritised at thresholds 0.05 to 0.5 (`05_prioritise.py`); the four phenotype associations hold under three alternative tests (`check/v11_tierA_fragility.py`); selection of C172 among five neuron-dominated components with CERAD FDR 0.011 to 0.013 (`ranking/`); the best-fitting t distribution (12 degrees of freedom), under which about 3 of the 76 few-donor components would be population-level components above 0.10 by chance (Figure S1a; `check/v07_prioritise_verify.py`).

Notes: `05_prioritise.py` still uses the cross-layer criterion (it reads `03_crosslayer.csv`); `reprioritise/p4_reprioritise.py` applies the rule without it, which leaves the 113 unchanged. `check/v03_gwas.py` writes `v03_gwas_547.csv`, then stops with a `KeyError` at its check of the `.fam` phenotype columns, as in the original run; later steps need only the table. `check/v11_tierA_fragility.py` is the check that the four phenotype associations hold under three alternative tests (Results): in the original analysis it was run inline after `v07`, and the script reproduces its log line for line; it also prints the q-value of each of the four associations under each test.
